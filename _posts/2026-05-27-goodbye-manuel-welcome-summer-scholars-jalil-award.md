On **May 27, 2026**, the HEART-GeN Lab gathered for a celebration that brought
together several important moments for our team.

<!--more-->

We said goodbye and thank you to **Manuel Hurtado, Jr.**, whose work, care, and
steady presence contributed meaningfully to the lab. Manuel has been an
important part of HEART-GeN, and we are grateful for everything he helped build
with us.

The gathering also served as a warm welcome for our **HEART-GeN Summer Scholars**.
We are excited to have them join the lab community and begin a summer of
research, mentorship, computational training, and scientific growth.

We also celebrated **Jalil Hemphill** winning the **Fletcher Prize for Rising
Undergraduate Research Star**, recognizing his commitment to undergraduate
research and the promise of the work he is developing in the lab.

These moments are what make lab community matter: honoring contributions,
welcoming new scientists, and celebrating the milestones that move each person
forward.
