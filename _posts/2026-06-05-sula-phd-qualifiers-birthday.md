On **June 5, 2026**, the HEART-GeN Lab had two reasons to celebrate **Sula
Perloff**: she successfully passed her **PhD qualifying exam**, and it was also
her birthday.

<!--more-->

Passing the qualifying exam is a major milestone in doctoral training. It marks
an important transition toward independent scholarship, deeper scientific
ownership, and the next stage of dissertation research.

Sula brings thoughtful questions, intellectual curiosity, and a collaborative
spirit to the lab. We are proud of the work she has put into this milestone and
excited to support the research she will continue building as a PhD candidate.

Congratulations, Sula, and happy birthday from the HEART-GeN Lab!
