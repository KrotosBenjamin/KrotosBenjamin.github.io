On **April 11, 2026**, the HEART-GeN Lab stepped away from the usual pace of
research for a team activity: **pickleball and lunch**.

<!--more-->

Lab life is shaped not only by experiments, code, meetings, and manuscripts,
but also by the time we spend building trust and community with one another.
This outing gave the team a chance to relax, move around, share a meal, and
connect outside the day-to-day rhythm of research.

We are grateful for moments like these that help sustain a collaborative and
supportive lab culture.
