The HEART-GeN Lab is proud to congratulate **Jalil Hemphill** on being selected
as the recipient of the **Fletcher Prize for Rising Undergraduate Research
Star**.

<!--more-->

The Fletcher Prize honors undergraduate researchers in the **Undergraduate
Research Assistant Program (URAP)** who have shown significant growth as
researchers, made meaningful contributions to faculty-mentored research, and
demonstrated strong potential to apply their research skills toward future
goals.

Jalil was nominated by his faculty mentor, **Dr. Kynon J. M. Benjamin**, and
selected by the URAP Committee, which includes faculty from across the
University. The committee reviewed nominations based on the skills and methods
students developed during the program, their contributions to faculty research
projects, and their promise as emerging scholars.

In his nomination, Dr. Benjamin highlighted Jalil's intellectual rigor,
quantitative aptitude, organization, responsiveness to feedback, and ability to
troubleshoot challenges systematically. These qualities have made Jalil an
important contributor to the HEART-GeN Lab and reflect the kind of careful,
thoughtful, and collaborative science we strive to support.

This recognition is a significant accomplishment and speaks to Jalil's hard
work, creativity, and dedication to undergraduate research.

We are excited to celebrate Jalil's achievement and look forward to seeing how
he continues to grow as a researcher.

Congratulations, Jalil!
